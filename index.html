How to play

Stack the falling tetrominoes to fill complete horizontal lines. Full lines clear and score points. Clear four at once (a Tetris) or pull off a T-spin for big bonuses. The game ends when the stack reaches the top.

Desktop controls

← →  /  A DMove left / right
↓  /  SSoft drop
SpaceHard drop
↑  /  XRotate clockwise
Z  /  CtrlRotate counter-clockwise
C  /  ShiftHold piece
P  /  EscPause

On phones

On-screen buttons appear automatically: a D-pad on the left to move and soft-drop, and rotate / hold / DROP buttons on the right.

Tips

  • The faint outline shows where the piece will land (ghost).
  • Use Hold to stash a piece for later — once per drop.
  • Back-to-back Tetrises and T-spins keep a +50% bonus going.
